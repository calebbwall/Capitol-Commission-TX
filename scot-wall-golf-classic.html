Scot Wall Golf Classic

About the Event

Capitol Commission Texas is a 501(c)(3) organization. The Scot Wall Golf Classic is our annual fundraising golf tournament benefiting the Capitol Commission Texas ministry serving Texas state leaders.

All proceeds directly support the ministry of reaching every person in the Texas State Capitol with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

TrackMan Contest

Test your skills with our TrackMan technology contest. Compete for prizes on the driving range.

Putting Contest

Show off your short game in our putting contest. Compete for prizes on the putting green.

4-Man Scramble

1:00 PM Shotgun Start. Compete as a team in our 4-Man Scramble format for a fun and competitive round of golf.

Support the Ministry

Your participation directly supports Capitol Commission Texas and our mission to minister to Texas state leaders.

For registration information, sponsorship opportunities, or questions about the event, please contact us.
